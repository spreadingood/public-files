Account Deletion

Effective Date: [Insert Date]

RealDate ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy and giving you full control over your personal data. This page explains how you can permanently delete your RealDate account and the data associated with it.

1. Deleting Your Account In the App

You can delete your account at any time directly from the RealDate app by following these steps:

  • Open the RealDate app on your device
  • Go to the Settings tab
  • Scroll to the About section
  • Tap the Delete Account button
  • Confirm that you want to permanently delete your account

Once confirmed, your account will be deleted immediately.

2. What Happens When You Delete Your Account

When you delete your account, the following data is permanently removed from our systems:

  • Your profile, photos, bio, and preferences
  • Your matches, swipes, and conversations
  • Your messages and chat history
  • Your notifications and account settings

After deletion, you will no longer be able to log in or access any of your data. Any content you sent to other users will no longer be available.

3. No Data Retention

We do not retain your account or personal data after deletion. When you delete your account, your information is permanently erased from our servers with no retention period. We do not keep backup copies of your profile, messages, or any other personal data.

There is no recovery option: once your account is deleted, it cannot be restored.

4. Unable to Access Your Account

If you cannot access your account to delete it in the app, you may request deletion by contacting us at: realdate@spreadingood.com

Please include the email address associated with your account so we can verify your identity. We will process deletion requests within a reasonable timeframe.

5. Your Rights (GDPR)

If you are located in the European Economic Area (EEA), you have the right to request the deletion of your personal data at any time. We will comply with such requests without undue delay and without retaining your data.

To exercise your rights, contact: realdate@spreadingood.com

6. Contact

For any questions about account deletion or your personal data, email us at: realdate@spreadingood.com
